Tips

For YouTube Shorts, use the 9:16 preset. Exports happen in real time, so a 60-second video usually takes about 60 seconds to render. Chrome and Edge typically work best. If MP4 recording is unavailable in your browser, the app exports WebM, which YouTube supports.
